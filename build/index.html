What the five days contain

A compressed senior product team.

The scope is deliberately sharp. That gives us enough time to make the complete core experience real, then make it feel considered.

  1. Day 1

    Find the product.

    Reduce the idea to its essential job, settle the interaction model, and cut everything that steals focus.

  2. Days 2 and 3

    Make the whole loop real.

    Direct agents across the interface, data, APIs, platform integration, and tests until the app genuinely works.

  3. Day 4

    Try to break it.

    Attack edge cases, failure paths, accessibility, performance, and the seams between systems.

  4. Day 5

    Make it feel inevitable.

    Refine typography, motion, copy, feedback, empty states, and the hundred small decisions that create trust.

  5. Handoff

    It is yours.

    You leave with the working app, its source, a clear account of what was built, and a sane route forward.

Needs a longer runway

  • Unresolved stakeholders or product strategy

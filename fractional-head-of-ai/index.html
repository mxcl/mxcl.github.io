Fractional Head of AI.

Standards, decisions, and pressure where it matters.

Some teams do not need another brainstorm. They need someone to set the operating model, reduce drift, and keep product and engineering pointed at systems that work.

  • → Quality bars

What the role changes

Set the operating model

  • ownership across product, engineering, and platform
  • release rules that do not move every week
  • practical boundaries on the roadmap

Get teams speaking plainly

  • less hand-waving
  • tighter prioritization
  • one definition of what good looks like

Normalize reliability work

  • evals become standard practice
  • failure handling stops being cleanup work
  • ownership becomes visible

Drive 90-day outcomes

  • visible movement
  • less advisory drift
  • clear accountability for change

Best fit

Scaling teams

  • AI is materially important now
  • the company is not ready for a full-time executive hire
  • coordination is getting expensive

Expected outcome

  • clearer accountability
  • fewer regressions
  • less time wasted on strategy theater

Fractional Head of AI

Senior AI leadership for the messy middle

Some teams do not need another brainstorm. They need standards, sharper decision-making, and someone who can connect product, engineering, and operational reality. That is the fractional role.

Direction

Set the operating model

Alignment

Standards

Normalize reliability expectations

Cadence

Drive 90-day outcomes

I prefer outcome-based mandates with visible movement rather than open-ended advisory drift.

Scaling teams

Clearer accountability

Better decisions, fewer regressions, and a sharper sense of what is strategic versus merely fashionable.

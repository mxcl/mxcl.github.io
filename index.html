Biography

Open source,
then and now.

Max Howell is a British-American software engineer and open-source developer. He created Homebrew and wrote its original code under the mxcl handle.

His other open-source work includes PromiseKit, Path.swift, and YOLOKit. His current work focuses on agent runtimes, secure execution, and production AI systems.
